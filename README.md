🌌 About the Mission

What if the key to building permanent human settlements on the Moon and Mars could be found right here on Earth?

EarthToMars is a NASA Space Apps Challenge project focused on identifying locations on Earth that share environmental, geological and geographical similarities with potential permanent human settlement locations on the Moon and Mars.

By exploring Earth’s extreme environments, we aim to identify natural analog sites that can help researchers understand the challenges of future extraterrestrial habitats.

Our mission is to connect Earth-based environments with the future of planetary exploration.

🎯 Mission Objectives

* 🌍 Identify Earth locations with Moon-like and Mars-like characteristics.
* 🌕 Explore environments that resemble potential lunar base locations.
* 🔴 Discover Earth landscapes that resemble Martian terrain.
* 🛰️ Compare geological and environmental conditions.
* 🗺️ Visualise potential analog locations geographically.
* 🚀 Support research into future permanent space settlements.

🪐 The Three Worlds

🌍 EARTH — Our Testing Ground

Earth provides extreme environments that can help us study the conditions future astronauts may encounter.

Potential analog locations include:

Location	Environmental Similarities
Atacama Desert, Chile	Extreme dryness and Mars-like terrain
Antarctica	Extreme cold and isolation
Hawaii, USA	Volcanic landscapes and rocky terrain
Arctic Regions	Ice, cold and challenging environments
Iceland	Volcanic terrain and geothermal activity

🌕 THE MOON — Future Lunar Habitats

Our project explores Earth environments that can help us understand:

* Lunar surface conditions.
* Extreme temperature changes.
* Rocky and cratered terrain.
* Permanently shadowed regions.
* Polar ice and potential water resources.
* Challenges of establishing permanent lunar habitats.

🔴 MARS — Future Martian Settlements

We investigate Earth environments that provide useful comparisons for:

* Dry and desert-like environments.
* Rocky geological formations.
* Extreme temperatures.
* Dust and challenging surface conditions.
* Potential habitat construction environments.
* Resource availability.

🛰️ Project Features

* Earth Analog Discovery: Identify potential Earth locations that resemble lunar and Martian environments.
* Location Comparison: Compare geological, geographical and environmental characteristics.
* Planetary Exploration: Explore the relationship between Earth, Moon and Mars environments.
* Interactive Mapping: Display and explore potential analog locations.
* Scientific Data: Use publicly available planetary and Earth observation datasets.

🧪 Technology & Data

Our project aims to explore scientific datasets and resources from:

* NASA Open Data
* NASA Earthdata
* NASA Planetary Data System
* USGS Astrogeology

🔭 Why This Matters

Before humanity can establish permanent bases beyond Earth, we need to understand how humans and technology will survive in extreme environments.

Earth provides valuable natural laboratories for studying environmental challenges that may be encountered during lunar and Martian exploration.

By identifying and comparing these environments, EarthToMars aims to help connect scientific research on Earth with the future of human space exploration.
